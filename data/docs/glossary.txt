Glossary of retrieval terms

Corpus: the full collection of documents that a retrieval system can search.

Chunk: a contiguous piece of a document that is indexed and retrieved as one unit.

Embedding: a dense vector of numbers that represents the meaning of a piece of text.

Hit rate at k: the fraction of evaluation questions for which at least one relevant
document appears among the top k retrieved results.

Mean reciprocal rank (MRR): the average, over all questions, of one divided by the rank
of the first relevant result. A question whose first relevant result is ranked third
contributes one third; a question with no relevant result in the list contributes zero.

Grounding: constraining a language model to answer only from the supplied context, and to
say so when the context does not contain the answer.

Citation: a marker in the answer, such as [2], that points to the numbered source the
statement came from.
